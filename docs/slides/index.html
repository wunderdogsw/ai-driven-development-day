Vague AC vs precise AC

Vague
“User can update their profile.”
Precise: one behavior each
AC1Given a logged-in user, when they save a phone number that isn't in E.164 format, then the save is blocked and the field shows “Invalid phone number”.
AC2Given a logged-in user, when they save their profile more than 2 times within 1 second, then the third save is rejected with HTTP 429.

What makes an AC precise

1One behavior per AC
2Concrete values, not adjectives
3Error cases and permissions included
4Out of scope written down
5Two people would agree on pass or fail
If you can't write the test, the AC isn't done.

“Must never” ACs

Examples say what should happen. Rules say what must never happen.

always equal

Cart total = sum of its lines

never below

A balance never goes negative

twice = once

Saving twice gives the same result as once

A property test makes up thousands of inputs and tries to break the rule. When it finds one, it shrinks it to the smallest failing case.
